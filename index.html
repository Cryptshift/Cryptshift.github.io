About

A bit about me

I'm a Computer Science student at UC San Diego with a focus on systems-level programming. I love working close to the metal — managing memory by hand, understanding how data structures behave under the hood, and writing code that's predictable and fast.

Most of my coursework and personal projects live in C. I find there's something satisfying about solving a problem with minimal abstraction and seeing exactly what the machine is doing.
